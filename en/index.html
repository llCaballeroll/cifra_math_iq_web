FAQ

What you’re wondering.

Is it free?

Yes. Every level is free to play. There are ads and, if you want, you can buy bolts or remove ads with a one-time purchase.

Is the IQ real?

It’s an entertainment estimate based on how you play: correct answers, stars, speed, consistency and help used. It is not a psychological test or a diagnosis.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Cifra works offline and without sign-up. Internet is only needed for ads and purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. There are no servers with player data. If you delete the app your progress is deleted; Remove Ads can always be restored.

What age is it for?

Everyone. Riddles go from easy to very hard, so there’s a challenge for kids and grown-ups alike.
