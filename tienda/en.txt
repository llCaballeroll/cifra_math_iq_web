## Full description (≤4000)
Cifra is a math riddle game that actually makes you think.

Every level hides a rule. Crack it, enter the missing number and move on. No memory tricks: here, the thinker wins.

ORIGINAL RIDDLES
• 120 levels across 5 chapters: Awakening, Patterns, Wit, Labyrinth and Mastery.
• 8 puzzle types: sequences, symbols, transformations, operators, grids, wheels, triangles and logic.
• 3 stars when you solve it with no mistakes and no help.

YOUR IQ, LIVE
• An animated brain, with eyes and glasses, that reacts right along with you.
• Find out which hemisphere is stronger: logic and calculation, or patterns and spatial vision.
• Hints and solutions cost you: IQ is earned without help.
• Share your card on WhatsApp or Telegram: “Think you can beat my IQ?”

MORE MODES
• Daily Challenge with a calendar and streak.
• 200 Cross Math puzzles: drag the tiles and make every equation work.
• Blitz: 60 seconds, combos up to ×5.

BOLTS ⚡
Bolts pay for hints and solutions. Earn them by solving well, with the Daily Challenge or by watching a video. Need them right now? They’re in the store. You can also remove ads with a single purchase.

No sign-up. Works offline. Clean design, zero distractions.

Cifra’s IQ is an entertainment estimate, not a psychological test.
